MITSgrub is a smart campus dining and mess management solution built to eliminate rush-hour congestion and dramatically reduce campus food waste. Developed as a project for the Google Antigravity Hackathon organized by VOX MITS, the app bridges the gap between students and campus food vendors.


Key Features
Canteen Pre-Booking: Browse the daily menu, place orders in advance, and pick up food seamlessly without standing in long queues during short breaks.
Hostel Meal Tracker: Allows hostellers to easily mark whether they will be eating daily meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner), giving mess administration exact headcounts ahead of time.


Advantages
Reduces Food Waste: Precise headcounts from hostel RSVP tracking allow kitchen staff to prepare exact portions, minimizing daily food wastage.
Saves Time: Eliminates peak-hour canteen queues, allowing students and faculty to get their meals instantly.
Optimized Inventory & Planning: Helps canteen vendors accurately predict daily ingredient requirements, lowering operational overhead.
Data-Driven Kitchen Operations: Provides kitchen managers with actionable insights into popular menu items and attendance trends.
